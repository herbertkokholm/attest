# Contributing to attest

attest is released under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the maintainer also offers it
under separate commercial terms. To keep both options open, the maintainer must
retain the right to relicense all code in the project.

Because of that, external contributions can only be merged under a Contributor
License Agreement (CLA) granting the maintainer the right to relicense the
contribution, including commercially. Until a CLA is in place, external pull
requests will not be merged.

Note: a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) alone is not sufficient here, as
it certifies provenance but does not grant relicensing rights.
